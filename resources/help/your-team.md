Open **Team** to see the people in your team and how many are active.

**What you can see (and why)**
- **People you invited**: their first name.
- **Deeper levels**: no names, only *"Someone Sara invited"* or *"Someone in Sara's team"*. This protects their privacy.
- Nobody sees your phone, email or how much you earn.

A team member who stops paying shows as not active. When they pay again you earn again.
