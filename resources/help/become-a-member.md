Membership costs **{membership_price} per month**.

**How to join**
1. Sign in and open **Home** in your account.
2. Tap **Join**.
3. Pay: {if wallet.spend}tap **Join from my wallet** if you have enough in your wallet{if payments.manual}, or {/if}{/if}{if payments.manual}send the money by Telebirr or bank transfer (the details are on the page), take a photo of the receipt and tap **Send receipt**{/if}.
4. Your home page says **Receipt sent · we are checking it**. We check it, usually the same day. Then it says **Active member**.

{if payments.manual}If the receipt can't be accepted, you see the reason on your home page and we send you a message. Just send a new receipt.{/if}

**Renewing** works the same way: tap **Renew**. We remind you before your month ends. After the end you have {grace_days} extra days to pay before your benefits stop.
