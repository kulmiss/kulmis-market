{if shop.coupons}- **Coupon**: in your cart, type the code and tap **Apply**. If it can't be used, the cart tells you why.
{/if}{if shop.gift_cards}- **Gift card**: in your cart, type the gift card code and tap **Use**. What is left stays on the card for next time. Gift cards work for {gift_card_days} days.
{/if}{if shop.gift_cards}- To give one: open **Gift cards** in the shop.
{/if}
