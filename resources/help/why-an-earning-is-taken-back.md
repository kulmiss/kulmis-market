If a payment is given back (a **refund**), the earnings from that payment are taken back from everyone who earned from it. In **Earnings** the line shows **Taken back**.

{if wallet}If you already used that money, your wallet can show a **minus** amount for a while: *"Money given back: your next earnings pay this first"*.{if wallet.cashout} You can't cash out until it is above zero again.{/if}{/if}

This is why new earnings wait {pending_days} days before they are ready.
