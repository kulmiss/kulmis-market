title: '"Was this you?" messages'
topic: Account safety
order: 2
keywords: new sign-in, message, password changed, security alert
---
We always send you a message when something important happens on your account: a new sign-in, a new password, a new email{if wallet.cashout}, a new cash-out account{/if}{if accounts.two_factor} or two-step sign-in turned on or off{/if}.

- **It was you?** Nothing to do.
- **It wasn't you?** Change your password at once (Security → Change password) and tap **Sign out everywhere**. Then contact us.
