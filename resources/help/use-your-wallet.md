1. Add products to your cart and tap **Checkout**.
2. Tick **Use my wallet**.
3. If your wallet has enough, the order is paid at once. {if payments.manual}If not, it pays a part and you send the rest by Telebirr or bank transfer.{/if}

Only **Ready to use** money can be spent.{if membership} You can also pay your membership from your wallet.{/if}
