- **Your link**: Home → *Your referral link* → **Copy**. Send it in a chat or post it.
{if referral.share_buttons}- **WhatsApp / Telegram**: tap the button and choose who to send it to.
{/if}{if referral.qr_codes}- **QR code**: friends can scan it with their phone camera.
{/if}{if referral.share_kit}- **Share kit** (More → Share kit): ready-made posts with your code, and a picture card you can download.
{/if}{if referral.product_links}- **Product links**: on any product page, tap **Share** to get a link to that product with your code.
{/if}
People can also type your code at sign-up{if shop} or at checkout{/if}.
