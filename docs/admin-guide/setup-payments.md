Customers pay by **Telebirr or bank transfer** and upload a photo of the receipt. Staff check it and approve. Members can also pay from their wallet.

1. Admin → **Settings** → **Payments**.
2. Type your real **Telebirr details** and **bank details** (bank, account name, account number). Customers see exactly this text on the payment page.
3. Save.

The setup checklist shows **Your Telebirr and bank details are saved** once they are not the example text any more.

**Card payments (Chapa)**: not part of this shop (your decision #56 in QUESTIONS.md). Adding them again is a new feature.

How to approve a receipt: see [Orders and payments](daily-orders.md).
