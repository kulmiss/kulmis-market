Every new earning first shows as **On the way**. After {pending_days} days it moves to **Ready to use**.

Why wait? In case the payment is given back (refunded) in those days.

When it is **Ready to use** you can {if wallet.spend}spend it in the shop{/if}{if wallet.cashout} or cash it out{/if}.

Each line in **Earnings** shows from whom, which payment and the day it will be ready.
