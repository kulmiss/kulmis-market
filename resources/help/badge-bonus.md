Team badges (Bronze and higher) pay a **bonus every month**. It depends on how many people you invited are **active members** for the whole month.

- Your own membership must be paid all month.
- The bonus is paid after the month ends, and is on the way for some days before it is ready.
- To keep the shop safe, bonuses have a monthly limit. Your home page shows the bonus to expect.
