Open **Track order**, type your **order number** (it starts with KM) and your **phone**, and tap **Track**.

You also get a message when we receive your payment, when we start making it, when it is on its way (with the courier and tracking number) and when it is delivered.

Signed in? All your orders are under **Orders** in your account.
