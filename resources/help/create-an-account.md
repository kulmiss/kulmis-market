1. Open the shop and tap **Sign up** (top right, the person icon).
2. Type your first name, last name and phone number. An email is optional.
3. Choose a password and tick **I agree to the terms**.
4. Tap **Create account**.

{if referral}If a friend gave you a link or a code, open their link first, or type their code in **Referral code**. Then you are part of their team.{/if}

**One phone number = one account.** If the shop says *"This phone number already has an account"*, tap **Sign in** instead. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
