- After your month ends you have **{grace_days} extra days** to pay.
- After that, **you don't earn** from your team until you pay again. Payments made while it was ended don't count for you later.
- Your team stays yours. Money that is already in your wallet stays yours.
- Pay again any time: Home → **Join**. Earning starts again from that moment.
