**Before your first cash-out**: Wallet → **Add account**. Add your Telebirr number or bank account. **The name must be the same as the name on your account.** For your safety a new or changed account can be used after {account_hold_hours} hours.

**To cash out**
1. Open **Wallet**.
2. Type the amount (at least **{cashout_min}**) and tap **Request cash-out**. Less than {cashout_min} ready to use? The page shows how much is ready; the form comes when you have enough.
3. We send it within **{payout_days} days**. You get a message with the reference.

**Limits**: up to {cashout_daily_count} cash-outs and {cashout_daily_max} a day.

**Why a cash-out can be held**
- It is the first cash-out of a new account: we check it once.
- The name on the account doesn't match.
- Something looks unusual (for example many accounts on one phone). Your money stays safe while we check.

If a cash-out is not approved, the money goes back to your wallet and we tell you why.
