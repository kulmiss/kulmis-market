---
title: If your account was hacked
topic: Account safety
order: 3
keywords: hacked, stolen, someone else, lost phone
---
1. Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and choose a new password.
2. In **Security**, tap **Sign out everywhere**.
{if wallet.cashout}3. Check your cash-out account in **Wallet**. A changed account can't be used for {account_hold_hours} hours, so you have time.
{/if}4. Contact us right away: {support_phone} or {support_email}.

Lost your phone with the code app? Use one of your **recovery codes** to sign in.
