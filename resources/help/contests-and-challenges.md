{if referral.contests}- **Contests** have dates and prizes for the top places. The leaderboard shows only first names and badges.
{/if}{if referral.challenges}- **Challenges**: for example *"Bring 3 new paying members this month"*. Reach it and you get the reward after the month.
{/if}{if account.goals}- **Goals**: on your home page, set *"Earn X this month"* and watch the bar fill up.
{/if}{if loyalty_reward}- **Loyalty reward**: {loyalty_reward} every {loyalty_months} months in a row as a member.{/if}
