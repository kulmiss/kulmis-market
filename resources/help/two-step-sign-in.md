Two-step sign-in asks for a code from an app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Authy…) after your password. Even if someone learns your password, they can't sign in.

1. Open **Security** in your account.
2. Tap **Turn on two-step sign-in** and scan the QR code with the app.
3. Type the 6-digit code.
4. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe. Each one works once if you lose your phone.
