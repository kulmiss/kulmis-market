Before you start, get these ready. You can add the last ones later.

| What | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| **A domain** (e.g. kulmis.market) | The address of your shop | Before you go live |
| **Hosting**: shared hosting (cPanel) with PHP 8.3 and MySQL, **or** a VPS (Ubuntu) | Where the shop runs | Before you go live |
| **HTTPS** (a free certificate from your host, or Cloudflare) | Safe sign-in; Telegram needs it | Before you go live |
| **An email address** for the shop, and its SMTP details (from your host) | Order and safety messages | Before you go live |
| **Your Telebirr number and bank account** | Customers pay by transfer and send a receipt | Before you go live |
| **A Telegram bot and a private channel** | Trading signals | Only if you use signals |
| **An SMS provider** | Messages by SMS | Later (email and Telegram work without it) |
| **Cloudflare** (free plan) | Speed and protection from attacks | Recommended |
| **A password manager** (Bitwarden, 1Password…) | Keep your passwords, recovery codes and backup key | Now |

> Online card payments (Chapa) are **not part of this shop**: you decided that customers pay by Telebirr or bank transfer with a receipt, and members can pay from their wallet. If you want card payments later, that is a new feature.
